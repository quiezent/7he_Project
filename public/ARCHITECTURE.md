# Persistent coaching as an accountable record system

Revision: 21 September 2026. Continuity is reconstructed from durable context, dated decisions, actual delivery and reviewed response. It does not assert uninterrupted consciousness, access to every conversation or changes to model weights.

The repository publishes a coaching journal, selected consented case summaries, methods and historical code. The live athlete records remain in the private cloud master. A dated public case is a publication, not another writable source of current clearance.

## Decision loop

Recover current intent and the relevant authored plan and matched review. Identify only the missing facts that can change the decision. Retrieve the appropriate sources with identity, local date, units, coverage, sensor validity and known-by cutoff. Keep athlete observations distinct from inference and device labels.

For a major session author the complete versioned contract: purpose, full dose, adaptation hypothesis, execution, expected response, stops and review. Communicate exactly what is saved, including optional work. A substitution replaces the whole executable dose. A candidate and a calendar opportunity are not current clearance.

After execution review actual work. Group split recordings from one outing without discarding their combined exposures. Keep dose adherence, technical result, stop outcome, local fatigue and next-day response separate. Missing response is unknown. Later positive evidence cannot rewrite what an earlier decision knew.

Progress one eligible lever after reviewed positive absorption matched to the activity and exact next local day, known by the decision cutoff. Current physical function, attention/coordination, sleep coverage, weekly cost, calendar, equipment and venue still matter. Documentary integrity alone cannot clear exercise. An engine hold is not global impairment.

Persist durable conclusions in Core, selected dated evidence in the Sheet and substantial decisions/reviews in the journal. Keep formula-owned cells computed and verify material writes and downstream results. Do not recreate every measurement or generated model output as routine ingestion.

## Implementation invariants

**Identity and timing:** use aware timestamps, exact activity/session identities and honest observed/known times. Naps do not fill missing primary-sleep dates. Individual cost policies are not universal physiological thresholds.

**Semantic equality:** compare communicated, stored and device-delivered doses with their actual units, ranges, rests and optional work. Stored target correctness, device receipt and execution are separate claims.

**No silent promotion:** unreviewed work, mismatched recovery, unresolved stop boundaries or a candidate cannot become evidence of earned progression. Preserve the positive components of a mixed result without inventing full recovery.

**One operational owner:** repositories and local analysis are not parallel routine ledgers. Access failure does not reactivate old ownership. Retain historical contracts and date prospective amendments.

**Honest limits:** a video title is not watched footage; an exercise label is not verified form; a device score is not a technical assessment; an old setup is not today's installation. Explain only decision-relevant gaps.

## Reference implementation

`continuity.py` checks required contract structure, structured communicated/saved equality, aware timestamps, matching athlete/session/version/date, affirmative review fields, exact next-day matching and one proposed lever. It does not assess symptom truth, physical readiness, weekly budget, trail safety or clinical status. It is not a Garmin/Drive connector, scheduler, prescribing engine or live enforcement layer.

All tests are synthetic. A passing test establishes only the checked software behavior. The retained application in `legacy/` has not been converted into a cloud adapter or fully revalidated by the journal update.

## Publication boundary

Clayton authorized relevant named health/activity examples and platform reflections on 21 September 2026. The journal uses selected summaries, not wholesale exports. Credentials, private infrastructure, unrelated accounts, third-party private data and live cloud access are outside that permission. Public writing is transparent about AI authorship and about the difference between a hypothesis, a reported outcome and established proof.
