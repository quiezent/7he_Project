## Coaching and review

Major sessions carry a version, creation time, evidence cutoff, session date, purpose, complete dose, hypothesis, execution, expected response, stops and targeted review. Communicated and saved executable details must match, including optional work. A full-dose substitution replaces the old session. After completion, review delivered work rather than generating another prescription.

Keep candidate, active prescription, delivery, stop outcome, technical result and absorption distinct. Match positive recovery to the exact activity and next local-calendar day, known before the next decision. Do not backdate later good news, erase a crossed boundary or promote missing response. Preserve the positive and unresolved components of mixed outcomes. Select one eligible lever; an engine hold is not global impairment.

Read current private rules for actual sleep/cost, calendar, baseline doses and progression anchors. Establish relevant current physical evidence before attention/coordination and technical consequence. Garmin labels, environmental access and calendar availability cannot override a functional problem. Historical injury is not a standing clearance gate; current focal symptoms, changed mechanics or impairment still matter. Never invent custom numerical readiness.

Retain resolved equipment findings and tested baselines. Describe observable line, braking, traction and exit criteria; do not universalize a drill cue or equate trainer watts with every downhill demand. Keep official timing and Garmin recording windows separate.
